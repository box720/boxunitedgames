Fishy Friends

Raise, breed and collect pixel fish in cozy tanks, from a little river tank to the deep sea.

Coming soon Google Play · Android

One tank at a time

Start with a river tank

Feed your first tetras and loaches, watch them grow and earn coins, and buy new fish from the shop. Specs, your helper fish, shows you around and hands out little jobs along the way.

Seven waters to open

Level up to open a shrimp tank, the sea, a coral reef, an outdoor koi pond, the deep sea and a sunlit coral lagoon. Fresh and salt water fish stay apart, just like the real thing.

Breed new colours

Happy fish lay eggs. Pick the parents with the matchmaker, hatch rare colours, and mix shrimp colours to find new ones. Grow living corals and plants, and watch the koi pond change with the seasons.

Fill your album

Every fish you raise goes in the album book, with prizes for each tank. Pen pals send bottle mail, the Tank Times prints the news each morning, and the public aquarium wants your best bred fish for its exhibits.
