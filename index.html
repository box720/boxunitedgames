About the studio

Box United makes cozy little games for kids and families. Our first game, Petbox, is a pixel pet garden made with the Godot engine, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Every pet, tree and toy in it is drawn pixel by pixel by the game's own code.

We build for families first. Petbox's pets rest while you are away, so a busy week never ends in a sad garden, and a grown-ups page behind a simple check can set a daily playtime limit and a bedtime. There are no ads and no accounts, it plays offline, and it collects no data: the whole garden lives on your device.
