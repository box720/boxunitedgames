Hatch a new friend

Hatch a Glimmling, a Drakelet, a Puffling or a Gillby, give it a name and make it feel at home. Rub its head, shake fruit from the trees and watch its personality show: some swing and nap, some kick the ball around, some splash in the pond or toast marshmallows by the campfire. Look after your pets and they'll greet you, learn tricks and make best friends.

Care shapes who they become

Kindness makes a pet Sunny; a little mischief makes it Moony. Sea Melons grow Splash, Sky Berries grow Soar, Dash Peppers grow Dash and Rock Nuts grow Might. When a pet grows up, it curls into a seed pod and blooms into a new form chosen by its stats and its nature. Raise it with lots of love and it may become Radiant.

Hybrids, Mystic and Divine

Pair two different kinds to hatch one of seven hybrids, from the horned Glimwyrm to the bubbly Bubblepuff. Lucky eggs hatch into five glowing Mystic lines. When an old friend has lived a full life, it visits the Glimmer Tree and reblooms as a new egg that keeps its name and memories. A deeply loved Radiant pet of the fourth generation can even take a Divine form.

Something every day

Pip, your garden guide, tells you what your pets got up to while you were away, and small daily quests, pet wishes, letters in the mailbox, hatch-day parties and Nana Bramble's Daycare give every visit something to look forward to.

Five cozy gardens

Start in the Cozy Oasis, then open the warm Sunny Glade, the lantern-lit Moon Hollow, the floating Cloud Isle and the underwater Coral Reef, each with its own trees, fruit and music. Day turns to night with your clock and the weather changes. The real seasons bring spring blossoms, autumn leaves and winter snow, and holidays bring special eggs such as the Pumpkling and the Frostling.

Race, train, show off

Earn belts in the Dojo with a training partner, then race over hurdles, through pools, off gliding cliffs and up climbing walls, from the Sprout Cup all the way to the Star League. Try the Cup of the Week, beat your best times and bonk your way through the Tumble Ring.

Every weekend the Garden Festival opens with the Charm Contest and the Cozy Contest, where three judges score the decor you arrange. Put a band of pets on stage at the Talent Show, with a new theme each week. Need a break? Play Pond Fishing, Fruit Catch or Hide-and-Seek.

Friends near and far

Visit the Friendship Plaza, where the town's gardeners drop by with their pets to race, play and swap little gifts. Share short codes with friends to send a pet on a Garden Postcard, show off your garden, trade pets or race a friend's ghost.

On the same Wi-Fi? Play Together lets two phones share one garden: toss the ball, send emotes and fill a fruit basket side by side. It only uses the Wi-Fi you are both on, never the internet, and the host decides who joins.

Help Hazel

Hazel Burrow, the toymaker mole, builds the swings, fountains and tiny carousels your pets really play on, and carves gold and crystal statues of your favorites in her workshop. One day her magic toy box, the Petbox itself, loses its four parts, and she'll need your help to find them.

For grown-ups

Calm, kind, private

Petbox is made for kids and families. Here is exactly what that means, in plain words.

Calm by design

  • Pets rest while you're away: no starving, no punishing timers, nothing lost.
  • A daily playtime limit and a bedtime in Settings > Grown-ups, behind a grown-up check. Both are off until you turn them on. When time is up, the pets yawn and tuck into bed, and nothing is lost.
  • Good-news reminders stay off unless a grown-up turns them on: at most one a day, never at night, only happy news like “Your egg hatched!”
  • Big text, music and sound volume, reduced motion, relaxed timing and vibration settings.
  • Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.

Privacy promises

  • No ads, no accounts, no data collection. Your garden is saved only on your device.
  • No chat: friends playing together send preset emotes, never typed messages.
  • Plays offline. Play Together links two phones on the same Wi-Fi only, never over the internet.
  • Pet names that come from other players pass a name filter on your device.
  • Nothing is for sale in the game. If that ever changes, purchases will sit behind a grown-up check.
