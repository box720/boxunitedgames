Petbox is a cozy pixel-art pet garden for kids and families. Hatch a Glimmling, a Drakelet, a Puffling or a Gillby, give it a name and look after it. How you care for a pet shapes who it becomes: kindness makes it Sunny, a little mischief makes it Moony, and the fruit you share grows its stats until it curls into a seed pod and blooms into a new form.

Five gardens follow your clock and the real seasons. There are Dojo belts and obstacle races, a festival every weekend, mini-games, and Hazel the toymaker mole, whose workshop builds the toys your pets really use. Pets rest while you are away, so nothing is lost if you take a break. Petbox plays offline, has no ads and no accounts, and collects no data.

Features

  • Hatch and raise four original pet species, seven hybrids and five glowing Mystic lines
  • Your care shapes them: Sunny or Moony nature, four stats, seed-pod blooms, Radiant pets
  • Rebloom at the Glimmer Tree, then reach a Divine form: Everbloom, Solar Seraph, Lunar Seraph or Guardian Spirit
  • Five gardens with day, night, weather and real seasons: Cozy Oasis, Sunny Glade, Moon Hollow, Cloud Isle, Coral Reef
  • Dojo belts with training partners, obstacle races from the Sprout Cup to the Star League, the Tumble Ring
  • A Garden Festival every weekend, a Cup of the Week, daily quests and pet wishes
  • Mini-games: Pond Fishing, Fruit Catch, Hide-and-Seek
  • Hazel's Workshop: recolors, pet statues, bigger gardens and a new special toy every week
  • Friendship Plaza, Garden Postcards and friend codes, plus Play Together on the same Wi-Fi
